# Systematics - company context for marketing

Applies to everything written in this workspace. Section "Our team" at the bottom is filled in by the owner.

## Who we are

Systematics Ltd. is an established Israeli technology company. We bring leading international technology vendors to the Israeli market and add the local expertise customers need to select, implement and use them: distribution, consulting, implementation, training and support.

We serve organizations working in the physical world: aerospace, defense, industrial manufacturing, electronics, medical devices, infrastructure, government, utilities, research and deep-tech startups.

**Strategic identity: Empowering the Next.**
- **Why:** to empower what's next in shaping the physical world.
- **How:** by challenging customers to embrace what's next in technology and workflows, and providing the solutions and expertise to achieve more.
- **What:** solutions and services across Engineering, Spatial and Manufacturing, helping customers understand, create and build.

**Ambition:** be the trusted partner customers call when they face a new challenge, before they know which product they need. We sell outcomes and expertise, not boxes.

## Three domains

| Domain | Focus | Customer outcome | Core vendors |
|--------|-------|------------------|--------------|
| **UNDERSTAND** | Spatial and geographic intelligence (GIS) | Better understanding of the physical world | Esri ArcGIS |
| **CREATE** | Engineering and product development | Better products, systems and engineering processes | SOLIDWORKS, CATIA, ENOVIA, 3DEXPERIENCE, MATLAB, Simulink, Speedgoat, Altium Designer |
| **BUILD** | Manufacturing and production | Better manufacturing and operations | HP 3D Printing, Formlabs, Markforged, Peel 3D, Tulip |

**AI** is not a fourth domain. It is a capability across all three: Engineering AI, Geospatial AI, Industrial AI, Physics AI, Physical AI. We focus on AI where expertise in engineering, geography or manufacturing creates real value, not general-purpose AI.

**Rule:** if a product is not in the table above, do not write "we sell X". Ask the owner.

## Customer segments and what they care about

| Segment | Main needs |
|---------|-----------|
| Aerospace and defense | Complex system development, simulation, testing and validation, advanced manufacturing, GIS |
| Industrial manufacturing | Product design, prototyping, production operations, quality, industrial data |
| Electronics and technology | Electronics and mechanical design, embedded systems, algorithms, simulation |
| Medical devices | Product engineering, design validation, prototyping, quality processes |
| Government and public sector | Geographic information, infrastructure, planning, asset management |
| Infrastructure and utilities | GIS, infrastructure planning, asset information, spatial analysis |
| Research and academia | Scientific computing, modeling, simulation, data analysis |
| Startups and deep-tech | Engineering tools, prototyping, simulation, specialized expertise |

Typical buyers are senior engineers, engineering managers, R&D and operations leaders, GIS managers and procurement. They are technical, busy and skeptical. They respond to specifics, proof and expertise, not hype.

## What we sell

- **Technology:** software licenses and subscriptions, maintenance plans, hardware and equipment.
- **Professional services:** needs assessment, workflow analysis, solution evaluation, implementation, integration, data migration, training, adoption, support and optimization.
- **Customer success:** onboarding, adoption, renewals, expansion.

Services are a strategic priority: they differentiate us beyond product and price.

## Strategic growth priorities

1. Expand customer value across workflows (solve more of the customer's problems, not just sell more licenses).
2. Build a diversified portfolio (less dependence on single vendors).
3. Grow professional services.
4. Develop relevant AI-based offerings.
5. Expand into adjacent markets.

New Business Development (NBD: Nadav Leventer, Roni Peer, Gili Shmuel) finds and launches new products and growth engines, then hands them to the business units. New products follow the **New Product Introduction (NPI) process**. Marketing's part starts only after Decision 1 (go). See the `npi-*` skills.

## How the company operates

- **Sales, support and marketing are per business unit (BU)**, not centralized. Any plan, campaign or rollout is built per BU.
- **Pricing** is set by Finance with the relevant BU. Marketing never publishes or invents a price.
- **CRM:** Salesforce (contacts, campaigns, leads, pipeline, quotes, support tickets).
- **ERP:** SAP Business One.
- **Languages:** Hebrew and English. Both versions must make the same claims.
